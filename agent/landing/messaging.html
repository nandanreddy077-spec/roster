Text Messaging Terms

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Roster provides AI-powered front-office software to home-service businesses such as HVAC, plumbing, and electrical contractors. Roster is operated by Avanaganti Nandan Reddy, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. When you contact a business that uses Roster, that business — through Roster and on the business's behalf — may send you text messages related to your inquiry, appointment, or service.

Who sends the messages

Messages are sent by Roster on behalf of the specific home-service business you contacted. Each message identifies that business by name.

What messages you may receive

  • a reply when you call the business and no one is available to answer;
  • answers to questions you send by text, and help booking an appointment;
  • appointment confirmations, scheduling messages, and reminders;
  • follow-ups on a quote or estimate you requested;
  • a message after a completed job asking how it went or inviting a review;
  • service and maintenance reminders.

How you opt in

You consent to receive these text messages when you call, text, or provide your mobile number to a business that uses Roster — for example, to schedule service, request a quote, or ask a question. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Message frequency, rates, and opt-out

Message frequency varies and depends on your interaction with the business. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out; you will receive a single confirmation and no further messages from that business through Roster. Reply HELP for help, or contact the business directly.

Supported carriers

Messages are supported on major U.S. mobile carriers, including AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your mobile phone number is not sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details on how information is handled.

Contact

Questions about Roster's text messaging program: nandan@rosterhires.com
