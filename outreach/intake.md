# When a business says YES — intake checklist

The moment someone agrees to a pilot, capture these on the spot. This is exactly the
`ClientConfig` the dashboard needs — once you have it, onboarding is filling in a form.

## Capture these 6 things
1. **Business name** — exactly as they want it said to customers.
2. **Trade** — HVAC / plumbing / etc.
3. **Services** — the 5–8 things they actually do (e.g. AC repair, furnace install, drain cleaning).
4. **Hours** — incl. whether they do 24/7 emergency.
5. **Pricing / FAQ** — anything the AI should know: diagnostic fee, "we don't quote over
   text," emergency surcharge, service area, brands they work with. Whatever they'd want
   a receptionist to know.
6. **Escalation** — the phone number/person to alert for a real emergency.

## Also ask (this is the gold — informs what to build next)
- **"What do you use to manage your schedule today?"** → Google Calendar? Jobber?
  Housecall Pro? Paper? (This decides the first integration we build — do NOT guess it.)
- **"What does an emergency mean for you?"** → defines urgency handling.
- **"Where do most of your calls come from?"** → Google, Angi, referrals, repeat customers.

## Then
- Add them as a client in the Roster dashboard (the 6 fields above).
- Point their line at Roster once the Quo registration clears.
- Watch the first real conversations closely; fix anything the AI gets wrong.
