Fair questions

The three things every owner asks.

"Won't my customers hate talking to a robot?"

Some would — so they don't have to. Anyone who wants a person gets you, immediately. For everyone else the real choice isn't robot versus human. It's a text back in five seconds versus your voicemail, and most people who hit voicemail just call the next name on Google.

"Do I have to change my number or my software?"

No. Keep your number. Keep whatever you already run the business on. Your office works alongside it and puts booked jobs on your calendar. Setup is our job, not yours.

"What happens when it gets something wrong?"

A human catches it — that's the product. Roster is managed: we read your conversations, fix wrong answers, and tune your employees every week. You're not buying software and hoping. You're hiring staff with a manager included.
