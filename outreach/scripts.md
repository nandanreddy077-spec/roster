# Roster — Outreach Scripts

Goal: one "yes" to a free 2-week pilot. Keep it short, personal, one business at a time.
Swap `HVAC`/`plumbing` to match the trade. Never mass-blast — send each one individually.

---

## Channel 1 — Google Maps "Message" button (use first if available)

Search the business on Google Maps → if there's a **Message** button, use it. Sends as
a text to their phone, routed through Google (no +91 shows). Best deliverability + trust.

> Hi! Quick question — when you're out on a job and a call comes in that no one can
> pick up, what happens to it right now?

(That's the opener. It starts a conversation instead of pitching cold. When they reply,
follow with:)

> Got it — that's exactly what I help with. I set up an AI that instantly texts those
> missed callers back, answers their questions, and books the job, so it doesn't go to
> a competitor. I'll set it all up for you, free, for 2 weeks — you only pay if it
> actually books you jobs. Want to try it on your line?

---

## Channel 2 — Email (for leads without a Maps message button)

**Subject:** Quick question about missed calls at {{business_name}}

> Hi {{owner_or_business_name}},
>
> When you're on a job and a call comes in that nobody can answer, where does it go
> right now — voicemail, or does it just ring out?
>
> I ask because missed calls are the #1 way home-service businesses lose jobs to a
> competitor who picked up first. I set up an AI that texts those callers back within
> seconds, answers their questions, and books the job for you.
>
> I'll set the whole thing up myself, free, for 2 weeks. You only pay if it books you
> real jobs — no setup fee, no commitment.
>
> Worth a 10-minute look? Just reply and I'll show you how it works.
>
> — Nandan

---

## If they say "how much?"
> Honestly, I'd rather prove it works first. Free for 2 weeks, then it's per booked
> job — so it only costs you when it's already made you money. Fair?

## If they hesitate / say no
> No worries — last thing: what would make something like this a no-brainer for you?
(Then just listen. That answer is the most valuable thing you'll get all week.)

---

## Daily rhythm (Monday on)
- Send 10–15 personalized messages/emails per day from the leads list.
- Log every reply in leads.csv (status: contacted → replied → interested → setup).
- Reply within minutes when someone responds — speed is the whole pitch.
