## SAY THIS FIRST (every call)
> "Hey, is this [Owner]? … [Owner], my name's Nandan. I'll be straight with you — this is

## THEN THE PERSONALIZED LINE (below, per lead) → let them answer → SAY THIS:
> "Right — and here's the thing. Most people who hit voicemail don't leave a message, they
> just call the next guy. So that's a job gone. What I do is set up an assistant that
> instantly texts those missed callers back — answers them, gets the job details, and books
> it for you. You don't change how you work. It just catches what you'd otherwise lose.
>
> And here's why I'm calling instead of emailing — I'll set the whole thing up myself, free,
> and run it two weeks on your line. You don't pay a dollar unless it actually books you real
> jobs. If it doesn't, you've lost nothing. Worth a try?"
>
> (If yes:) "Awesome. 10 minutes to grab your services, hours, and pricing so it sounds
> exactly like you — today or tomorrow morning?"
